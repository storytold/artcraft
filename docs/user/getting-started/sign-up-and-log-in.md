When you start the desktop app, a window asks you to sign up or log in. You cannot close it without doing one of them.

## Sign up

1. In the window, find **Create your account**. If you see **Welcome back** instead, click **Sign up** at the bottom.
2. Fill in **Username**, **Email**, **Password** and **Confirm Password**. The password needs at least 8 characters.
3. Click **Sign up**.
4. The app can then show **Join Our Community** with a Discord invite. Click **Join Discord** or **Skip for now**.
5. Click **Get Started**.

If the two passwords do not match, the app shows "Passwords do not match."

## Log in with a password

1. In the window, find **Welcome back**. If you see **Create your account**, click **Log in** at the bottom.
2. Enter your email or username in **Email or Username**, and your **Password**.
3. Click **Log in**.

## Log in through the website

Use this if you signed up with Google or are already signed in on the website.

1. On the **Welcome back** window, click **Login with Website** to open your browser, or **Scan to Login** to use your phone.
2. The app shows a QR code and a code. Approve the login on the website and check that the codes match.
3. Wait. The app shows "Waiting for your confirmation on the website…" and then "Signed in successfully."

The code expires. If the app says "Login request expired. Start a new request.", start again with **Login with Website** or **Scan to Login**. If you decline on the website, the app says "Login was declined on the website."

## Log out or switch accounts

1. Click the **Settings** button in the top bar.
2. Open **Accounts**.
3. Next to **ArtCraft Account:**, click **Log Out**, or **Log In** if you are logged out.

## If sign-up fails

Sign-up can fail without a clear reason. Issue [#2004](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/2004) is open. In it, a user was told the email was already taken when they had never signed up. One person got in by resetting their password on the website and then approving the login in the app. The team has not confirmed this fix.

To get help:

1. Check that you typed the email correctly.
2. Try **Login with Website** instead of the password form.
3. Ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft), or add your case to issue [#2004](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/2004).

## Still stuck?

Ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft) or open an issue in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues).
