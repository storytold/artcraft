Credits pay for generations that run through ArtCraft. You can see your balance in **Settings**, under **Plan & Credits**. Prices are on [getartcraft.com/pricing](https://getartcraft.com/pricing).

## Do I have to pay?

No. The pricing page says you can use ArtCraft without a subscription, and that you can bring your own compute and third-party subscriptions. See [Use your own provider accounts](connect-provider-accounts.md).

**Settings** > **Plan & Credits** also says: "You do not have to purchase anything from us to use ArtCraft, but you can support ArtCraft development by subscribing or buying credits."

## Two kinds of credits

- **Monthly credits** come with a paid plan and refill every month.
- **Purchased credits** are one-time packs you buy. The pricing page says credit packs never expire.

Both are added together in your balance.

## Free plan

The app lists a Free plan for accounts without a subscription. Its plan card mentions free daily generations and limited access to ArtCraft tools. How many free generations you get is not documented yet, so this page does not give a number. Check your balance in **Plan & Credits**.

## Check your balance

1. Click the **Settings** button in the top bar.
2. Open **Plan & Credits**.
3. Read **Your ArtCraft credit balance**. Below it, **Monthly credits (refilled monthly)** and **Purchased credits** show the two parts.

## Buy credits

1. Open **Settings**, then **Plan & Credits**.
2. Click **Buy credits**.
3. Pick a one-time pack. No subscription is needed.

## Subscribe or change plan

1. Open **Settings**, then **Plan & Credits**.
2. Click **Upgrade plan**. If you already have a plan, the button says **Change plan**.

To cancel, click **Cancel plan** in the same place. It only shows when you have a plan you can cancel.

Plans are Basic, Pro and Max. The pricing page also lists Enterprise, where you contact the team. See [getartcraft.com/pricing](https://getartcraft.com/pricing) for what each plan costs and includes.

## What a generation costs

Cost depends on the model and your settings. Some pages have a **Costs** button. It opens a cost calculator and shows the estimated credits for the current settings.

The pricing page says credits are refunded if a generation fails.
