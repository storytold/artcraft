---
title: Find, download and delete your creations
description: Open My Library, filter by type, download files to your computer, and delete one or many items.
order: 70
verified: 0.41.0
---

Click **My Library** in the top bar to see everything you have made and uploaded. Click an item to open it, then use **Download** or **Delete**. On a narrow window the button shows only an icon.

## Find an item

1. Click **My Library**.
2. Use the list on the left to filter by type: **All Assets**, **Image**, **Video**, **Audio**, **Mesh**, **Splat** or **Uploaded**.
3. At the top, **All Assets** shows your files and **Folders** shows your folders.
4. Click **Refresh list** if a new item is missing. **Toggle image fit** switches between cropped and full thumbnails.

Uploads are hidden by default in **All Assets**. Turn on **Show uploads** to see them, or use the **Uploaded** filter.

To sort items into folders, click **New folder** on the **Folders** tab. To file an item, open its menu and choose **Add to Folder**.

## Download an item

1. Open the item from **My Library** by clicking it.
2. Click **Download**.

Where the file goes depends on your download settings. Click **Settings**, open **Downloads**, and check:

- **Default Download Directory**: the folder files are saved to. **Choose Directory** changes it and **Use Default** resets it.
- **Ask location before download**: when on, a file picker opens each time so you choose the folder.
- **Auto Download**: when on, finished generations are downloaded to your computer as soon as they complete.

## Delete an item

1. Open **My Library**.
2. Hover the item and open its menu, or right-click it. Choose **Delete**. You can also open the item and click **Delete**.
3. In the box titled "Delete this media?", click **Delete**. Click **Cancel** to keep it.

Deleting removes the item from your library for good. The confirmation says this cannot be undone.

## Delete many items

1. In **My Library**, click the small checkbox at the top left of each item you want. The checkbox shows when you hover an item.
2. A bar appears at the bottom with **Add to folder** and **Delete**. Click **Delete**.
3. Confirm in the box that asks "Delete N items?".

Click the X at the right of the bar to clear your selection.

## If delete fails

ArtCraft shows an error message when an item cannot be deleted. Deleting generated images from the library was broken in older versions ([issue #969](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/969), closed in January 2026). Update to the latest version if you still see it. If it persists, ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft) or open an issue in the [issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues).

## Other things you can do with an item

Open an image and the same panel can also show **Recreate**, **Edit Image**, **Make Video**, **Remove BG**, **Make 3D**, **Make 3D World** and **Share**. Which buttons show depends on the item. **Share** copies a link to the item.
