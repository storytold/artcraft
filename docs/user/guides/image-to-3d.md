Open **Image to 3D Object** from **Home**, add one image, and click **Generate Object**. When the model is ready you can view it, open it in 3D Stage or download it as a GLB file.

## Steps

1. Go to **Home** and click the **Image to 3D Object** card. The page is titled "Generate 3D Object".
2. Click the **Add Image** tile. Hover it to see **Upload image** and **Pick from library**. You can also drag an image onto the tile.
3. Choose a model with the model picker next to the button. See [Models](../reference/models.md).
4. Click **Generate Object**. The credit cost shows on the button.

The button is disabled until you add an image and it finishes uploading.

## Start from an image you already made

- Open the image in **My Library** and click **Make 3D**.
- In [3D Stage](3d-editor.md), click the wand button next to **+** (tooltip "Create 3D model from image").

## Use the result

After you click **Generate Object**, the page splits into a viewer and a **History** list.

- Click an entry in **History** to view it. An entry reads "Generating..." while it runs.
- **Open in 3D Editor** adds the object to your scene in **3D Stage**.
- **GLB** downloads the model as a GLB file.
- **Clear Session** empties the **History** list.

Click **Costs** at the bottom right to see the credit cost. See [Credits and pricing](../getting-started/credits-and-pricing.md). If a generation fails, ArtCraft shows "Failed to generate 3D model" with the reason.
