**Image Editor** is a canvas for laying out images, shapes and brush strokes. When you click **Generate**, ArtCraft uses the canvas as input along with your prompt. Open it from **Image Editor** in the top bar.

## Start a canvas

You begin on a start card with three choices:

- **Select Image**: upload an image from your computer. You can also drag an image onto the card.
- **Pick from Library**: use an image from [My Library](library.md).
- **Blank Canvas**: choose a size in **Choose Canvas Size**, then click **Create Canvas**. Sizes are grouped as Square, Landscape and Portrait.

## The toolbar

The toolbar on the left has these tools. Hover a button to see its name.

- **Select & Move**: pick and move items.
- **Add Shape**: draws a rectangle, circle or triangle. Choose the shape from the small menu next to the button. When a shape is selected, a colour button appears above the toolbar.
- **Upload Image**: adds an image to the canvas.
- **Brush**: draws freehand. Choose a colour and set **Brush Size** in the panel that opens.
- **Mask**: paints a mask for edits. It only works with models that support masks. See [Edit an image with a prompt](edit-an-image.md).
- **Eraser**: erases. Set **Eraser Size** in its panel.
- **Canvas Background**: sets the canvas colour.
- **Undo** and **Redo**.
- A red X button at the bottom of the toolbar, **Reset Canvas**. It asks "Are you sure you want to reset the canvas?" and clears all your work if you click **Reset all**. This cannot be undone.

The button corners show the keyboard shortcut for some tools. You can change shortcuts in **Settings**, under **Keybinds**.

## Add images from your library

Open **My Library** and drag an image onto the canvas. You can also use **Upload Image** in the toolbar.

## Work with items

Right-click a selected item to open a menu with **Remove background**, **Lock**, **Bring to front**, **Bring forward**, **Send backward**, **Send to back**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**. A locked item shows **Unlock** instead.

Click **Fit** in the prompt box to fit the canvas to the window.

## Generate

1. Choose a model in the prompt box.
2. Type a prompt in the box that says "Describe your image...". The canvas already carries your layout, so a short prompt is enough.
3. Click **Generate**.

Results appear in the history stack. Click one to make it the base image. Click **Costs** at the bottom right to see the credit cost.

## Tips

- High-contrast sketches give clearer guidance.
- Mask small areas for local edits such as logos, eyes or hands.
