Not yet. ArtCraft generates with cloud services today. There is no setting to point it at a local model or a ComfyUI install.

## What the team has said

- ComfyUI: a team member replied on [#968](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/968) that this is "100% going to be put on our roadmap" and that they want ArtCraft to talk to Comfy. Later commenters asked for updates. The issue is open and has no date.
- Local model weights: [#1147](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/1147) asks for local Qwen image edit. A team member replied "we'll be working on this soon!" The issue is open and carries the `local models` label.
- The [roadmap](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) does not mention ComfyUI or local models by name. It lists "Remove dependence on ArtCraft hosted services" under Architecture, which is not the same thing.

These are replies in the issue tracker, not a release plan. This page does not promise a date.

## What you can do now

- Use your own Grok or Midjourney account instead of ArtCraft credits. See [Use your own provider accounts](../getting-started/connect-provider-accounts.md). These are still cloud services.
- Add your voice to [#968](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/968) or [#1147](https://github.com/storytold/artcraft/issues/1147), or ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/artcraft).
